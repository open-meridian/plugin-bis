# Terms

A summary, not legal advice: read the BIS's own words before relying on
them, and settle your use with your counsel. Terms change; these were read
on 18 November 2026.

## The BIS's terms

From the BIS's legal page for its data
([data.bis.org/help/legal](https://data.bis.org/help/legal)):

- **Use with the BIS cited.** The BIS's statistics may be used provided the
  BIS is cited as the source, a translation is marked as unofficial, and no
  endorsement by the BIS is implied.
- **No added charge.** Including them in a commercial product must not add
  a charge for them to that product's users. Whether your product meets that
  is your firm's decision.
- **No other use** is permitted.
- **The API as is.** The API is offered as is, and may be suspended,
  versioned or limited.

The policy rates are each central bank's, as the BIS compiles them; the
long consumer price series are the BIS's compilation from national sources.

## In your deployment

Each dataset describes these terms in words, shown on the Data sources
page, and declares no licence. The licence a deployment admin sets there is
the one enforced; until one is set, the lake keeps the rows and reads every
use right restrictively. The plugin says nothing of whether your deployment
meets anyone's terms.
